## Narration-only text

*(Everything below is the same script with direction stripped — paste this straight into a
voice tool.)*

A small moon. Eight outposts. One craft. Something is descending on all of them at once.

This is Mare Noctis — and its entire design comes from one piece of arithmetic: on a
sphere, you cannot be everywhere.

Harvesters land on an outpost and drain it. You fly to one — and the far side of the
world is genuinely far away. So every second you spend defending here is a second you did
not spend defending there. The game never asks you to win a fight. It asks you which one
you are willing to lose.

Enemies are beaten, not just survived. An Interceptor stalks you, then winds up for a
second — it stops manoeuvring, lines up, and visibly flares. Then it commits to a dive it
cannot correct. Break that line and it blows past you into two full seconds of exposure,
where it takes two and a half times damage and cannot shoot back.

Damage lands on a system, not a number. A hit degrades your engine, your weapon bay, or
your stabiliser — and a hurt stabiliser pulls your nose one consistent way that you fly
against, by hand, for the rest of the wave.

Press H and the bay launches an escort that flies your wing and engages on its own. The
first sortie is one drone, for twenty seconds. Bring the formation home alive and the next
launch is bigger and longer — up to four. Lose even one, and you start again from a single
drone. So a formation of four is something you earned across four careful sorties. And it
is at its most valuable exactly when it is most likely to die.

Perks build across a run. Being destroyed takes all of them. And then it offers you three
legendaries, and lets you fit one. The lowest point in a run is also the biggest decision
in it.

It runs in a browser tab, and there are no art files. Every crater, every enemy, every
surface is generated in code.

Underneath: a fixed 120-hertz simulation with interpolated rendering, so it plays
identically on a sixty-hertz laptop and a one-forty-four-hertz monitor. Zero React
re-renders while you fly. No allocation in the frame path, so the collector never runs
mid-fight. Every run is a seed and an input log — which means any run can be replayed,
exactly.

Eight outposts. One craft. You cannot be everywhere.

Mare Noctis. Playable now, in your browser.
